# Banned AI words and phrases

Source: Grammarly common AI words + stop-slop phrases.

## High-frequency
delve, delve into, underscore, underscored, pivotal, realm, harness, illuminate

## Transitions
that being said, at its core, to put it simply, this underscores, a key takeaway, from a broader perspective

## Softeners
generally speaking, typically, tends to, arguably, to some extent, broadly speaking

## Academic
shed light, facilitate, refine, bolster, differentiate, streamline

## Buzzwords
revolutionize, innovative, cutting-edge, game-changing, transformative, seamless integration, scalable solution

## Stop-slop extras
here's the thing, here's what, here's why, it turns out, let me be clear, the truth is, full stop, let that sink in, this matters because, navigate (challenges), unpack, lean into, landscape, deep dive, moving forward, circle back, in today's, it's worth noting, at the end of the day, when it comes to, in a world where, the reality is

## Mechanics
- No em dashes
- No adverb crutches: really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually, deeply, truly, fundamentally
- No "not X, it's Y" binary contrasts
- Prefer active voice and specific numbers
